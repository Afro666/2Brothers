BACKUP · WHAT AN APPROVAL LEAVES BEHIND

Every yes leaves proof

A signed receipt

It names the offer, the exact total, the lines and who approved. One public key checks it; change one number and the check fails.

A hash-chained audit log

Each line carries the hash of the line before. A changed line breaks the chain; a cut or rewritten end is detected against a head record.

BACKUP SLIDE: the demo’s beat 5 as one picture, and the answer when a technical judge asks "what if someone cuts lines off the end of the log?". SAY: Every approved order gets a signed receipt: the offer, the exact total, the lines, who approved. Anyone can check it with one public key. And every step is in a hash-chained log in MongoDB Atlas: change a line and the chain breaks, and a cut or rewritten end is detected against a head record. The receipt also says plainly that nothing was sent to the supplier. DO NOT SAY "anchored": the head record is not signed yet (issue #1886, open). Say "a cut or rewritten end is detected against a head record".
