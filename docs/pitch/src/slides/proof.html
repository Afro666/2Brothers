BACKUP · WHAT AN APPROVAL LEAVES BEHIND

Every yes leaves proof

A signed receipt

It names the offer, the exact total, the lines and who approved. One public key checks it; change one number and the check fails.

A hash-chained audit log

Each line carries the hash of the line before. A changed line breaks the chain; a cut or rewritten end is detected against a head record.
