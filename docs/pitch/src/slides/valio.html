SAY (20 seconds, one word to remember: VALIO): The video showed the kitchen’s side. This is Valio’s side. A kitchen can share its forecast with Valio, or not. Then Valio sees the demand against its own stock, before any order comes in. Five products short next week, and Valio knows on Sunday. LONGER, FOR A VALIO JUDGE: A restaurant chooses whether Valio may see its forecast, and it can switch that off again. With it, Valio sees what all those kitchens will need, per product and day, against its own stock, before a single order comes in. Its own agent then offers a substitute first. So the restaurant gets a buyer, and Valio gets the demand signal. And the same open interface could serve any wholesaler and its kitchens. THE PITCH TO VALIO, in one line: "We built this tool for you too." SAY IT STRAIGHT IF ASKED: the demand on this page comes from 20 demo kitchens with a synthetic history, built from real Valio products and prices. Valio Aimo is the only real supplier today, so "any wholesaler" is the next step, not built: that is why its stamp is dashed. The forecast was checked: over 10 weeks our engine misses 4.7% a week across the 20 demo kitchens, against 11.4% for a plain weekday average.
